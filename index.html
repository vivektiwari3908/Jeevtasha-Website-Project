ABOUT US

Empowering Communities
Through Compassion & Action

Jeevtasha Foundation is committed to creating positive social change through education support, food donation drives, environmental initiatives, healthcare awareness, and volunteer engagement across India.

Our mission is to uplift underprivileged communities while building a future driven by empathy, sustainability, and equal opportunity for everyone.
